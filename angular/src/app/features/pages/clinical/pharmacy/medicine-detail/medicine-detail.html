USP Verified Formulary

Active Ingredient & Strength:

Acetaminophen (Paracetamol) 500 mg per film-coated tablet. Non-narcotic analgesic and antipyretic agent.

Therapeutic Indications:

For temporary relief of mild to moderate pain including headache, muscular aches, backache, minor arthritis pain, toothache, and fever reduction.

Dosing Guidelines & Administration Limits

  • Adult Dosing: 500 mg to 1000 mg orally every 4 to 6 hours as needed for pain or fever.
  • Maximum Daily Limit: Do not exceed 3,000 mg (6 tablets) in any 24-hour period to prevent liver damage.
  • Special Populations: Reduce dosage in patients with severe hepatic impairment or chronic alcohol intake.

Safety Precautions & Black Box Warnings

  • Hepatotoxicity Warning: Acetaminophen has been associated with cases of acute liver failure, at times resulting in liver transplant and death. Avoid combining with other OTC/prescription acetaminophen products.
  • Alcohol Interaction: Chronic alcohol users (≥3 drinks/day) face heightened risk of liver injury.
