Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about appointments, billing, insurance and visiting hours

Appointments

Go to Appointments → Book Appointment, select your preferred department and doctor, then choose an available time slot. You'll receive a confirmation by email and SMS.
Yes, appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time from My Appointments, free of charge.
Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and any prior medical records or referral letters.

Billing & Insurance

We work with most major insurers including BlueCross, Aetna, Cigna and UnitedHealth. Visit Insurance → Claims to check eligibility for your specific plan.
Open Billing → Invoices, select the invoice and choose your preferred payment method — card, bank transfer or wallet.
Most claims are reviewed within 5–7 business days. You can track live status from Insurance → Claims.

Visiting Hours & Policies

General wards allow visitors from 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. ICU visiting is limited to immediate family, 15 minutes per slot.
Yes, our Emergency department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with dedicated on-call physicians and ambulance dispatch.
