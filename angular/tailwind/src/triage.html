ESI Reference Guide

Emergency Severity Index, 5-level triage scale

  • ESI 1

    Resuscitation

    Requires immediate life-saving intervention. Unresponsive, intubated, apneic, or pulseless.

  • ESI 2

    Emergent

    High-risk situation, severe pain or distress, or confused / lethargic / disoriented. Cannot wait.

  • ESI 3

    Urgent

    Stable, but two or more resources expected (labs, imaging, IV fluids, specialty consult).

  • ESI 4

    Less Urgent

    Stable, one resource expected (a single X-ray, simple laceration repair, or prescription).

  • ESI 5

    Non Urgent

    Stable, no resources expected beyond a focused exam. Prescription refill, suture removal.
